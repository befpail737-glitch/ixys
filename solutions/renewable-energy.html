Renewable Energy Systems

High-efficiency solar inverters and wind power conversion systems using IXYS power semiconductors for maximum energy harvest and grid integration.

Solution Overview

Our renewable energy solutions leverage IXYS power semiconductors to deliver high-efficiency solar inverters and wind power conversion systems. These solutions maximize energy harvest while ensuring reliable grid integration with advanced power electronics.

Applications span residential, commercial, and utility-scale solar installations, as well as wind turbine power conversion systems. The solutions are designed for high power density, maximum efficiency, and long-term operational reliability under harsh environmental conditions.

Core Advantages

High Efficiency

IXYS power semiconductors achieve >98% conversion efficiency with advanced topologies.

Maximum Power Point Tracking

Advanced MPPT algorithms maximize energy harvest under varying irradiance conditions.

Grid Compliance

Meet international grid-tie standards with clean power quality and fault ride-through capability.

Reliability

Robust design with IXYS components for 25+ year operational lifetime.
