Industrial Motor Control Solutions

High-performance variable speed drives using IXYS power semiconductors for precise motor control in demanding industrial applications.

Solution Overview

Our industrial motor control solutions leverage IXYS power semiconductors to deliver high-efficiency variable speed drives for AC and DC motors. These solutions provide precise speed and torque control while maintaining high reliability in harsh industrial environments.

Applications include manufacturing equipment, conveyor systems, pumps, fans, compressors, and specialized industrial machinery requiring variable speed operation. The solutions are designed for high power density, efficiency, and long-term operational reliability.
