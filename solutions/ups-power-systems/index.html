Technology Solutions

IXYS Power Semiconductors for UPS Applications

Our UPS and power system solutions integrate IXYS' leading power semiconductor technologies designed specifically for power protection applications:

XPT™ Series MOSFETs: For high-efficiency UPS topologies, our XPT™ series provides optimized gate charge and low switching losses, enabling superior efficiency across varying load conditions.

Application Optimized IGBTs: For high-power UPS systems, IXYS' Application Optimized IGBTs feature characteristics tailored for UPS applications with optimized turn-off characteristics and enhanced ruggedness.

HiPerFET™ Series: For high-frequency UPS designs, our HiPerFET™ series provides superjunction technology for ultra-low conduction losses in battery charging and inverter applications.

Each solution is supported by comprehensive technical documentation, design tools, and application engineering support to accelerate your UPS system development.
