Technology Solutions

IXYS Power Semiconductors for Renewable Energy

Our renewable energy solutions integrate IXYS' leading power semiconductor technologies designed specifically for energy conversion applications:

HiPerFET™ Series MOSFETs: For high-frequency solar inverters, our HiPerFET™ series provides ultra-low RDS(on) and superior switching characteristics, maximizing energy harvest while minimizing losses.

Trench Field Stop IGBTs: For high-power applications in solar string inverters and wind converters, our Trench Field Stop technology provides low saturation voltage, soft switching characteristics, and enhanced ruggedness.

Superjunction Technology: For high-efficiency power conversion, IXYS' superjunction technology delivers exceptional performance in high-voltage applications with reduced conduction losses.

Each solution is supported by comprehensive technical documentation, design tools, and application engineering support to accelerate your renewable energy system development.
