UPS & Power Systems

Uninterruptible power supply and backup systems using IXYS power semiconductors for continuous power in critical applications.

Solution Overview

Our UPS and power systems solutions leverage IXYS power semiconductors to deliver reliable backup power systems with high efficiency and fast response times. These solutions ensure continuous power delivery during grid outages or disturbances.

Applications include data centers, telecommunications facilities, medical equipment, industrial processes, and other critical systems requiring uninterruptible power. The solutions are designed for high reliability, energy efficiency, and rapid switchover from AC to battery backup.

Solution Diagram

Core Advantages

Zero Transfer Time

Seamless switching from AC to battery backup with no interruption to critical loads.

High Efficiency

IXYS power semiconductors maintain >95% efficiency in double conversion mode.

Battery Life Optimization

Intelligent charging algorithms extend battery life and performance.

Reliability

Robust design with IXYS components ensures 24/7 operation for critical applications.
