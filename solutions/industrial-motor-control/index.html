Advanced Motor Control with IXYS Power Semiconductors

Our industrial motor control solutions leverage IXYS' advanced power semiconductors to deliver high-performance, energy-efficient control systems for a wide range of industrial applications. From variable frequency drives (VFDs) to precision servo systems, we provide tailored solutions that optimize efficiency, performance, and reliability.

With IXYS' Trench Field Stop IGBTs and Application Optimized IGBTs, we enable variable frequency drives with exceptional performance, reduced acoustic noise, and superior control characteristics. Our solutions address the most demanding requirements in industrial automation, heavy machinery, and manufacturing equipment.

Technology Solutions

IXYS Power Semiconductors for Motor Control

Our industrial motor control solutions integrate IXYS' leading power semiconductor technologies designed specifically for motor drive applications:

Application Optimized IGBTs: IXYS offers specialized IGBTs optimized for motor drive applications with tailored gate charge characteristics and soft turn-off behavior to reduce acoustic noise during operation.

Trench Field Stop IGBTs: For high-power applications, our Trench Field Stop technology provides low saturation voltage, soft switching characteristics, and enhanced ruggedness.

Polar™ Series MOSFETs: For high-frequency servo applications, our Polar™ series provides low RDS(on) and excellent switching characteristics.

Each solution is supported by comprehensive technical documentation, design tools, and application engineering support to accelerate your development process.
