About LiTong Group

LiTong Group is an official authorized IXYS distributor, providing genuine IXYS power semiconductors with comprehensive technical support and global sales network.

Our Mission

At LiTong Group, our mission is to be the premier distributor of IXYS power semiconductors, delivering genuine products and exceptional technical support to customers worldwide. We are committed to helping our customers achieve design success through comprehensive product knowledge, application expertise, and reliable supply chain management.

Our History

Founded in 2005, LiTong Group has grown from a regional distributor to a global partner for IXYS products. Over the years, we've built strong relationships with customers in diverse industries including industrial automation, renewable energy, automotive, and consumer electronics. Our growth has been driven by our commitment to technical excellence and customer service.

Our partnership with IXYS began in 2008, and since then, we've expanded our technical capabilities and global presence to serve the growing demand for high-performance power semiconductors.

Our Values

Integrity

We maintain the highest standards of integrity in all our business relationships, ensuring genuine products and transparent communication.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in everything we do, from product selection to customer service and technical support.

Innovation

We embrace innovation in our approach to solving customer challenges and staying ahead of industry trends.

We build long-term partnerships with our customers, suppliers, and distribution partners based on mutual success.

Our Team

LiTong Group's success is driven by our talented team of professionals who combine deep technical expertise with business acumen. Our team includes:

  • Field Application Engineers with extensive experience in power electronics design
  • Technical sales professionals with deep product knowledge
  • Supply chain specialists ensuring reliable product availability
  • Business development experts with global market insights

Our team members have decades of combined experience in the power semiconductor industry, with many having worked directly with power electronics design before transitioning to distribution and support roles.
