Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our IXYS distribution services, products, technical support, and ordering process.

How do I verify that I'm purchasing genuine IXYS parts from LiTong?

LiTong Group is an official authorized distributor of IXYS products. All parts are purchased directly from IXYS or authorized sources, and we provide full traceability documentation with every shipment. We also offer authenticity guarantees and have quality certifications including ISO 9001:2015.

What technical support do you provide for IXYS products?

Our team of Field Application Engineers provides comprehensive technical support including design assistance, application guidance, and troubleshooting. We offer reference designs, simulation models, and can assist with complex technical challenges related to power semiconductor implementations.

What IXYS product families do you distribute?

We distribute the complete range of IXYS power semiconductors including Power MOSFETs, IGBTs & Modules, Thyristors (SCRs), Rectifier Bridges, and Diodes. This includes various series such as Polar™, XPT™, HiPerFET™ for MOSFETs, and Trench Field Stop IGBTs.

What is the typical lead time for IXYS components?

For stocked items, we typically ship within 1-3 business days. For non-stocked items, lead times vary by product and quantity but are typically 4-8 weeks. We provide real-time inventory information and can provide specific lead times upon request. Our team can also work with you to provide forecasts and stocking solutions for your production requirements.

Can you help with design-in of IXYS components?

Yes, our Field Application Engineers provide comprehensive design-in support including circuit design, component selection, thermal analysis, and layout recommendations. We offer reference designs and application notes, and our team is available for direct technical consultation on your specific applications.

Do you stock IXYS samples for evaluation?

Yes, we maintain a comprehensive sample inventory of popular IXYS components for evaluation and prototyping. You can request samples directly through our website or contact our sales team. We offer sample quantities for most product families to support your design process.

What quality certifications does LiTong hold?

LiTong Group maintains ISO 9001:2015 quality management certification. We follow rigorous quality control processes and are an official IXYS authorized distributor. All our products meet RoHS compliance requirements and we provide certificates of compliance with every shipment.

What are your minimum order quantities?

Minimum order quantities vary by product, but we offer flexible ordering options including single-piece quantities for prototypes and development work. For production quantities, MOQs depend on product availability and supplier requirements. We work to accommodate customer needs across all order sizes.

Do you provide application-specific support for power systems?

Yes, our technical team provides application-specific support for various power systems including motor drives, renewable energy systems, UPS, and power supplies. We have reference designs and technical expertise in these specific application areas and can assist with system-level design challenges.

How do I find the right IXYS component for my application?

We recommend using our online product selection tools or contacting our technical support team. Our FAEs can help you select the appropriate components based on your voltage, current, frequency, and thermal requirements. We also provide application guides and technical articles to help with component selection.

Can't find the answer you're looking for?

Our technical team is ready to help with your specific questions about IXYS products and applications.
