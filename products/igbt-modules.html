IXYS IGBTs & Modules Overview

IXYS IGBTs (Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistors) and modules represent advanced power semiconductor solutions that combine the advantages of MOSFETs and bipolar transistors. These devices offer low conduction losses, fast switching speeds, and excellent thermal performance in a single package.

Our product range includes standard IGBTs, intelligent power modules (IPMs), and application-optimized modules. All products feature robust construction and are designed for high-reliability applications in harsh environments. Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between IGBT modules and discrete IGBTs?

IGBT modules integrate multiple dies in a single package with optimized thermal properties. Discrete IGBTs are individual devices in standard packages, suitable for lower power applications.

How do I select the right IGBT for my motor drive?

Consider the voltage rating (at least 1.5x bus voltage), current rating (based on motor requirements), switching frequency, and thermal management. Our FAE team can assist with selection.

What is soft saturation in IGBTs?

Soft saturation is a characteristic of IXYS IGBTs that provides a more controlled turn-off with reduced oscillation, resulting in lower EMI and improved system performance.
