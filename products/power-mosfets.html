IXYS Power MOSFETs Overview

IXYS power MOSFETs represent cutting-edge technology in high-voltage and high-current semiconductor solutions. These devices are engineered for superior performance in power switching applications, featuring advanced trench and superjunction technologies that provide low RDS(on) values, fast switching speeds, and excellent thermal characteristics.

Our key series include the Polar™ series for high-voltage applications, the XPT™ series for optimized performance, and the HiPerFET™ series for high-power density applications. All products are available with comprehensive technical support and application guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between XPT™ and HiPerFET™ series?

The XPT™ series uses advanced trench technology optimized for performance, while the HiPerFET™ series employs superjunction technology for ultra-low RDS(on) values, making it ideal for high-power density applications.

Can I get technical support for IXYS MOSFETs?

Yes, as an official IXYS distributor, we provide comprehensive technical support including design assistance, thermal analysis, and application guidance.

How do I choose the right voltage rating?

Always select a MOSFET with a VDS rating at least 20% higher than your maximum operating voltage to account for voltage spikes and ensure reliable operation.
