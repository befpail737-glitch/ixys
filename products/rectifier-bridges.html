Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a rectifier and a fast recovery diode?

Standard rectifiers are optimized for low forward voltage drop in 50/60Hz applications. Fast recovery diodes have much shorter reverse recovery time, making them suitable for high-frequency switching applications.

How do I calculate the power dissipation for a rectifier?

Power dissipation ≈ VF x IF(AV). For more accurate calculations, consider switching losses in high-frequency applications and thermal derating based on operating temperature.

What is reverse recovery time (trr)?

Reverse recovery time is the time required for a diode to transition from forward conduction to blocking state. Fast recovery diodes have shorter trr values, reducing switching losses in high-frequency applications.
