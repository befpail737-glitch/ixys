IXFN50N10P3 - IXYS Polar™ Series Power MOSFET

100V, 50A, 6.5mΩ RDS(on) - TO-247-3 Package

Key Specifications

  • Part Number: IXFN50N10P3
  • Series: Polar™
  • Configuration: N-Channel
  • Drain-Source Voltage (VDS): 100V
  • Continuous Drain Current (ID): 50A
  • RDS(on): 6.5mΩ (max)
  • Package: TO-247-3
  • Technology: Trench
  • AEC-Q101 Qualified: Yes

Product Description

The IXFN50N10P3 is a high-performance N-Channel MOSFET from IXYS' Polar™ series, designed for applications requiring high current handling with low on-resistance. With an industry-leading 6.5mΩ RDS(on) and 50A current rating, this device offers exceptional performance in high-power switching applications.

As part of the Polar™ series, the IXFN50N10P3 is optimized for applications in automotive, industrial motor drives, DC-DC converters, and power supplies. The device features low gate charge (Qg) and optimized figure of merit (FOM) for high-frequency operations while maintaining excellent thermal characteristics.

Applications

  • DC-DC Converters
  • Motor Drives
  • Power Supplies
  • Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS)
  • Electric Vehicle Charging
  • Industrial Equipment
