IXGN15N60 - IXYS Trench Field Stop IGBT

600V, 15A, 1.7V VCE(sat) - TO-220 Package

Key Specifications

  • Part Number: IXGN15N60
  • Series: Trench Field Stop
  • Type: IGBT
  • Collector-Emitter Voltage (VCE): 600V
  • Collector Current (IC): 15A
  • VCE(sat): 1.7V (max)
  • Package: TO-220
  • Technology: Trench Field Stop
  • Applications: PFC Circuits

Product Description

The IXGN15N60 is a mid-voltage IGBT from IXYS' Trench Field Stop series, specifically optimized for PFC (Power Factor Correction) applications. With a 600V rating and 15A current capability, this device offers exceptionally low saturation voltage (1.7V) and optimized gate charge characteristics for high-efficiency PFC circuits.

As part of the Trench Field Stop series, the IXGN15N60 provides superior performance with reduced switching losses and soft turn-off characteristics. This makes it ideal for boost PFC applications in power supplies where high efficiency and power density are critical requirements.

Applications

  • Power Factor Correction (PFC) Circuits
  • SMPS Power Supplies
  • AC-DC Converters
  • Charger Applications
  • LED Drivers
  • Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS)
