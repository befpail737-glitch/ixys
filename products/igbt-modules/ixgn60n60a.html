IXGN60N60A - IXYS Trench Field Stop IGBT

1200V, 60A, 1.8V VCE(sat) - TO-247 Package

Key Specifications

  • Part Number: IXGN60N60A
  • Series: Trench Field Stop
  • Type: IGBT
  • Collector-Emitter Voltage (VCE): 1200V
  • Collector Current (IC): 60A
  • VCE(sat): 1.8V (max)
  • Package: TO-247
  • Technology: Trench Field Stop
  • Soft Switching: Yes

Product Description

The IXGN60N60A is a high-performance IGBT from IXYS' Trench Field Stop series, featuring advanced trench technology combined with field stop design for optimized performance. With a 1200V rating and 60A current handling capability, this device provides low saturation voltage (VCE(sat) of 1.8V) and soft switching characteristics.

As part of the Trench Field Stop series, the IXGN60N60A offers superior performance with reduced switching losses. The device combines the advantages of MOSFETs (voltage control) and bipolar transistors (low conduction losses) in a single package. Its soft saturation characteristic provides controlled turn-off with reduced oscillation, resulting in lower EMI and improved system performance.

Applications

  • Switch-Mode Power Supplies (SMPS)
  • Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS)
  • Motor Drives
  • Induction Heating
  • Solar Inverters
  • Welding Equipment
